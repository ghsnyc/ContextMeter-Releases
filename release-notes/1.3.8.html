ContextMeter 1.3.8

Released October 2026

  • macOS 26 (Tahoe) fix — the menu bar popover could open with its top cut off above the screen. It now opens correctly under the menu bar icon, and scrolls if your screen is short.
  • Settings tabs — on macOS 26 the Settings tabs were hidden behind a "»" overflow menu. They're now a visible tab bar at the top of the window again.
  • New Claude models — added Sonnet 5.5, Opus 5.5, Haiku 5.5 and Fable 5.1 with current context window and pricing, so usage and cost estimates are right from the first message.
  • Bug fix — the Settings tab showed stray text next to the hook version, and ContextMeter could not upgrade its own hook scripts when a newer version shipped inside the app. Both fixed.
